# Eddy — Claude Context

## What this app is

A minimal music utility for voice leading guided by oblique strategies. Users move individual voices by small intervals — the chord emerges as a byproduct. Inspired by Brian Eno's Oblique Strategies. Target: songwriters/composers. Tagline: "follow the current".

## Stack

- Vue 3 `<script setup>`, Ionic, Pinia, Tone.js (instrument samples self-hosted in `public/samples/` — see CREDITS.md), @tonejs/midi
- Capacitor iOS platform live (`ios/`, bundle ID `com.yindad.eddy`); Android not yet added. Build/run: `npm run build && npx cap sync ios`, then build the `App` scheme in Xcode or via `xcodebuild`
- No backend — all client-side

## Key constraints

- MIDI range: E2(40)–C6(84). Spread ≤40 semitones. Seed zone E2(40)–B3(59), voiced open at the bottom (seedMinGap() in sequenceStore.ts: the lower the note, the wider the gap above it — a fifth for an octave-2 bass, a minor third below E3, a second below C4; close clusters only higher up). Streams can drift down to E2 over a flow. Picker range is instrument-specific (see useAudioEngine.ts INSTRUMENT_NOTE_RANGE); voice-leading engine stays on the global range regardless of instrument.
- No voice crossing (sorted ascending always). No chord names — purely voice movement.
- Always sharps (C#, F#, etc.)
- 3 or 4 voices only (5 = V2)

## Architecture

- `src/data/notes.ts` — MIDI constants, NOTE_NAMES, midiToName(), dissonanceRank()

- `src/data/velocityPatterns.ts` — Paul's accent-pattern list (guitar/piano × 4/4,3/4; cells of L/M/S, tagged 'pass' = voice pattern, one note per cell slot, or 'bar' = phrase shape tiled over the bar's slots), FAMILY_LEVELS (L .85 / M .65 / S .45), VELOCITY_FAMILY (instrument → family; unlisted instruments stay flat)

## Critical patterns

- **Capacitor iOS**: `fetch()` on `capacitor://` returns status 0 / `ok=false` with an intact body, so Tone's own sample loader rejects everything — `useAudioEngine.ts` fetches + decodes buffers itself (`loadBuffers`). MIDI export uses Filesystem (cache dir) + Share on native, `<a download>` on web. Scrollable `ion-segment` paints blank labels in WKWebView sheets — don't use `scrollable` there. Ionic overlays (`ion-action-sheet`, `ion-alert`) need `!important` on theme variables. `swipeBackEnabled: false` in main.ts's `IonicVue` config — the iOS edge-swipe-back gesture was competing with in-tray range sliders near the screen edge (tempo/ambience), occasionally sliding SessionView aside and triggering the "start fresh?" guard. Verify anything native-looking in the simulator, not just headless Chromium.

- **The mood chooser** (2026-10-20, replaces the key/scale vocabulary as the channel's front door for beginners): `settingsStore.moodId` + `applyMood(id, fitNotes?)`/`rollMoodRoot(fitNotes?)`. A mood owns the scale; the root is rolled — at every "let it flow" (fully random), at "flow from here" (best fit to the hand-entered source), and when picked mid-session (best fit to the current cluster). The engine still only sees keyLocked/keyRoot/scaleId. Hand-picking a key clears moodId. Home/session show the mood word instead of "D dorian"; saved flows carry an optional `moodId` (display only; the saved keyRoot is kept on load, not re-rolled). Home's "some notes sit outside…" note is suppressed for moods (the root is fitted at start).
- **The channel (key/scale lock)**: `settingsStore.keyLocked`/`keyRoot`/`scaleId` (+ `keyChosen`, so the session lock icon can re-lock a remembered scale in one tap). Per-app-run, not part of saveAsDefault; saved with a flow (`ChannelState` in sessionStorage.ts, optional for old saves). It constrains *movement* — a hand-entered source is never blocked, only noted — and `randomStart(voiceCount, channel?)` seeds from in-scale notes (scale-aware fallback chord: stacked 1-3-5-7). The lock is **strict**: every note a move lands on is in the scale. The two strategies that deliberately leave the key (`KEY_LEAVING_STRATEGY_IDS` in strategies.ts: parallel-mode, resolve-wrong) are held out of the deck entirely (kept in code for a future explicit "cross the channel" feature); the other three `requiresKeyLock` strategies stay in-scale and only enter the deck while locked. In `generateCandidates()`, strategies "settle": only the candidates with the fewest out-of-key notes survive, so held strays (hand-entered source, a mid-flow key change, a hand-edited stream) get pulled back in; measured ~1% of locked-flow clusters carry a stray. Session lock icon toggles + regenerates streams; the dim scale line under the header opens the picker. Per-pool locks and a pool key label were deliberately not built (pools hold already-generated rows; a label would be wrong for mixed pools) — see the design conversation 2026-10-15.
- **Static outer voices — analysis, and the moved-notes marking that was tried and removed** (2026-10-23): Paul reported the top (and bass) note often runs through many chords. Measured (300 flows × 40 chords, neutral picker): the engine is NOT inner-biased (each outer voice moves ~41–44% of moves, each inner voice 26–40%), but a top note held 4+ chords occurs in ~98% of 40-chord flows (longest streak mean ~6; 13–34% of flows hold 8+), the default ½–1 step drift keeps a 40-chord flow to ~8 distinct top/bass notes (~6 key-locked), and 4 of the 15 non-key-lock strategies never move the top (top-stays, drop-the-third, rise-by-half, slash-chord) and 4 never move the bass. **Tried and reverted:** dimming held notes / bolding moved notes in each stream pill — Paul found it disrupted the visual flow, and a user toggle wasn't worth another control. **Also tried and removed (2026-10-23):** a stalled-voice nudge in the offered streams of manual flows (reserve 2 of the 6 slots for streams that move a top/bass held 2+ rows) — the sampler already offers a mover in ~79% of stalled moments (avg 2.6 of ~5 streams move the stalled voice, 3.3 when the strategy offers any); the nudge added ~0.2 streams and small, inconsistent long-stall gains; the other 21% is the strategy itself holding that voice (hold-bass etc.), which Paul chose to leave alone. (The starting-streams chain keeps its own version of the idea — there the app does the picking, and it works.) Ideas still open: a mild "stuck voice" nudge (simulated: a voice held ≥3 chords gets 2 of the 6 stream slots → flows with 8+ same-top-note streaks 46% → 30% neutral picker / 50% → 24% smooth picker), "top walks"/"bass walks" strategies, outer voices drifting farther than inner ones.
- **Starting-streams chain avoids stalled outer voices** (2026-10-23; `pickChainStream()` in HomeView.vue): when the starting-streams count is >1, each chained row is picked at random from the offered streams, except that once the top or bass has held the same note for 2 rows (`CHAIN_STALL_ROWS`, lowered from 3 on 2026-10-23 at Paul's request for more movement; simulated static-top-4+ 29% -> 21%, static-bass 30% -> 19%, no ping-pong increase; the stall rate is the same at every drift setting — drift only changes how far the outer notes travel: top range over 8 rows ½ step 1.7 st / ½–1 step 2.8 / 1 step 3.6 / ≤3rd 3.4 / free 6.4) the pick prefers streams that move it (both, if both are stalled). Only the app's own picks in a chain are affected — never the streams offered, never a manual pick. Measured: 8-row chains with a 4+-row static top 47% -> 29% (real app, 3 voices; simulation predicted 27%), static bass 46% -> 27%, both 17% -> 7%; it breaks stalls but doesn't widen the range (distinct tops ~3.2 -> 3.3), and strategies that offer no mover (top-stays) can still stall. If the drawn strategy offers nothing that moves a stalled voice (hold-bass, top-stays, add-extension never move the bass…) `chainOptionsFor()` draws up to 4 other strategies until one can — leaving ~1% of stalls unfixed instead of ~22% (a sampling gap was ruled out: 0% of misses could have been fixed by sampling the same strategy again). What remains is almost all (93–98%) both-outer-voices-stalled-at-once, where only one can be moved unless a strategy moves both; hunting for a both-mover was simulated (4-row chains, 1-step drift: same-bass-3-in-a-row 20% -> 11%, but both outer voices moving on the same step 16% -> 20%) and NOT built. Real app, 4 voices, 1 step, 4 starting streams: same bass 3 in a row 38% (before) -> 23%. Manual flows are unchanged.
- **The "spacing" setting** (2026-10-23; `settingsStore.spacing`, `src/data/spacing.ts`; close / normal / open, a segment between voices and drift in Home's "the current" sheet; a default saved with defaults like voices/drift, not with a flow): how spread out the chords are. Applies when a new flow starts (`randomStart(voices, channel, spacing)`: close caps the seed spread at 9 (3v) / 11 (4v), open widens each step to an octave and requires ≥15 (3v) / ≥17 (4v), up to 24 / 27; normal is the original code path, untouched) and as a **soft bias** in `sampleCandidates()` (4 of the 6 stream slots go to candidates inside the band when enough exist — a lean, not a wall). Measured (300 flows, keys lock on/off): median start spread normal 9 / 11 (3v/4v), close 8 / 10, open 18 / 22; **close vs normal differs by only ~1 semitone in width** — its real effect is higher register (octave-2 bass starts 46% -> 16% for 3v, 27% -> 5% for 4v, 0% when key-locked) and a narrower top end (p90 13 -> 9 / 14 -> 11); the dissonance gate and seedMinGap already prevent true clusters, so close can't go much tighter without relaxing them. Under the default ½–1 step drift the spread holds for 32+ moves; under 'free' drift 3-voice flows drift back toward normal (4-voice stay clearly open). Streams per move are unchanged (~5.0 / 3.6 key-locked).
- **The "drift" size setting** (`movementSize`: ½ step / ½–1 step / 1 step / ≤ 3rd / free, in Home's "the current" sheet) is wired into `generateCandidates()` as `options.movement` (a per-voice semitone range: half 1, step 1–2, whole exactly 2, third 1–4, free no cap), applied before the key-lock settle and sampling; strategies whose character is a leap just yield nothing under a tight cap and the redraw picks another. **It was never wired before 2026-10-20** — shown and saved since the first commit but never read, so every earlier flow behaved as 'free'. Default is 'step' (½–1 step) — Paul's call 2026-10-20 after hearing it; a device that already ran "save as default" keeps its stored value. Trade-off measured: tighter caps make flows loop back sooner (median moves to a repeated cluster with random picks: free 17, ≤3rd 15, ½–1 step 13, ½ step 9, 1 step 7), and ½ step + a scale lock has few options (~1.5; major scales have few half steps).
- **Strategy card bug fix**: `activeStrategy` is a local ref in SessionView, set atomically in `advance()` — never use `currentStrategy` from the composable directly in the template
- **Loop playback**: Use Transport.loop (not Part.loop). Set all loop params BEFORE `loopPart.start(0)`. Start transport with `'+0.05'` offset.
- **randomStart()**: Uses retry loop (30 attempts) + guaranteed fallback cluster — never passes invalid cluster to start()
- **start()**: Always clears state first before validating — prevents stale session data leaking
- **Single start button**: HomeView shows "let it begin" OR "begin here" (v-if/v-else on showManual) — never both at once
- **Direction/tempo/subdivision/latch/timeSignature/instrument changes during playback**: watchers in SessionView call playLoop() (restarts cleanly) — structural settings, need a clean restart
- **Ambience is different**: live-rampable (`setAmbience()` ramps the existing Tone.Reverb/Chorus `.wet` Signal, no restart) — its dial ceiling is roughly double each instrument's original always-on wet value, with the settingsStore default of 0.5 landing exactly back on the original, pre-dial sound (see the REVERB_SETTINGS comment in useAudioEngine.ts for the math). **UI hidden** as of 2026-09-29 (`SHOW_AMBIENCE_CONTROL = false` in SessionView.vue) — didn't earn its screen space — but the store field/live ramp/save-restore all still work, just no slider to change it mid-session. Flip the flag to bring it back.
- **Tempo +/- buttons**: tap moves exactly ±1 bpm (every value must be reachable, not just multiples of 5); holding accelerates the step size the longer it's held (see startTempoHold() in SessionView.vue)
- **Footer tray row layout**: a flex row's `margin-left: auto` child does nothing unless that row's own container has real slack to give it — `.toggle-row`/`.tempo-control` both need explicit `flex: 1` (or `flex-basis: 100%` to force an unconditional line break, not just one that happens to trigger at narrow widths) for this to work. Bit twice already (latch-btn's placement, then the time-signature toggle's).
- **MIDI export time signature**: `@tonejs/midi` has no `setTimeSignature()` — push directly to `midi.header.timeSignatures` and call `midi.header.update()`, or a receiving DAW (Logic Pro, etc.) silently assumes 4/4 regardless of how the notes are laid out.
- **Audio export / Tone.Offline()**: `Tone.Offline()` temporarily swaps the *global* Tone context for the duration of the render — anything reading `Tone.getContext()` while a render is in flight (e.g. live playback's per-frame tick loop) would observe the offline context instead. `exportAudio()` in SessionView.vue stops live playback first rather than risk the race. AudioBuffers decoded on the live context are safe to reuse inside the offline one (AudioBuffer isn't tied to a BaseAudioContext, unlike AudioNode) — `renderSequenceToBuffer()` relies on this to avoid a second sample-loading path.

- **FILTER_SETTINGS** (`useAudioEngine.ts`) — per-instrument lowpass filter mechanism, `buildEffectsChain()`'s most-upstream stage (closest to the source, ahead of chorus/delay/reverb, so the reverb tail is warmed too, not just the dry signal). Not dial-scaled by Ambience — same "harder problem, out of live-control scope" reasoning DOWNRIVER.md gives for reverb decay. Currently empty (no instrument uses it) — built for, and proven out on, the shelved guitar experiment above; kept as ready infrastructure same as DELAY_SETTINGS.
- **Pad instruments force 'chord' direction**: `PAD_INSTRUMENTS` set + a watcher in SessionView.vue auto-selects chord and disables the other 4 direction buttons (and latch, via its existing chord-disable binding) whenever the active instrument is pad-type — a slow pad swell reads as muddy when arpeggiated. Remembers and restores whatever direction was active before, same precedent as latch surviving a trip through chord mode.
