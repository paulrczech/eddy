Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Eddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no backend server of any kind — everything Eddy does happens entirely on your device.

What Eddy stores, and where

Any flows you save, and your default settings, are stored locally on your device only (in your browser's local storage, or the app's local storage on iOS). This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is not accessible to us in any form. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes it completely.

Exporting a flow

When you export a flow as a MIDI file, that file is created on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet (or downloaded directly in a browser) so you can save or send it wherever you choose. Eddy does not upload it anywhere itself.

No analytics, no tracking, no ads

Eddy contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-party resources

Eddy loads one web font (Cormorant Garamond, from Google Fonts) over a standard HTTPS connection. No other third-party service is contacted while using the app.

Children's privacy

Since Eddy collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Eddy adds a feature that does involve a server — this page will be updated first, with a new date above.

Contact
